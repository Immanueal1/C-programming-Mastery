# ❓ Top 10 Technical Interview Theory Questions

> Core technical interview questions covering pointer definitions, memory address concepts, operator differences (`&` vs `*`), wild pointers, Call by Value vs. Call by Reference, pointer-to-pointer indirection, format specifiers, and pointer aliasing.

---

### Question 1
What exactly is a Pointer? Explain using the analogy of houses and street addresses.

---

### Question 2
Why are pointers necessary in C? Couldn't we just pass variables around normally?

---

### Question 3
Explain the difference between the Address-Of Operator (`&`) and the Dereference Operator (`*`).

---

### Question 4
What happens when you declare a pointer without initializing it? What is a wild pointer?

---

### Question 5
Explain the concept of "Call by Value". Why does it fail when trying to swap two variables?

---

### Question 6
Explain "Call by Reference". How does it solve the limitation of functions only returning a single value?

---

### Question 7
What is a Pointer-to-Pointer (`**`)? In what scenario might you need to use one?

---

### Question 8
Explain the "Square Trap" (modifying a copy vs. modifying the original).

---

### Question 9
What does the `%p` format specifier do? Why do we use it instead of `%d` for memory addresses?

---

### Question 10
Is it possible to have multiple pointers pointing to the exact same memory address? If one modifies the value, what do the others see?
