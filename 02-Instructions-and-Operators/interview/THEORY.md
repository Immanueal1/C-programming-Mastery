# ❓ Top 10 Technical Interview Theory Questions

> Core technical interview questions covering instruction types, assignment constraints, precedence, associativity, casting, modulo sign rules, and logical truth values in C.

---

### Question 1
What are the three main types of instructions in a C program?

---

### Question 2
Explain the "Left Hand Side" (LHS) rule in an assignment operation. Why does `a + b = c;` fail?

---

### Question 3
What is Operator Precedence, and why does C need it? Provide an example.

---

### Question 4
When operators share the same precedence (e.g., `*` and `/`), how does the compiler decide which to evaluate first? What is this property called?

---

### Question 5
Explain Implicit Type Conversion. What happens when you divide an `int` by a `float`?

---

### Question 6
How does Explicit Type Casting differ from Implicit Type Conversion? Give a syntax example.

---

### Question 7
Why is `^` not the power operator in C, and what does it actually do? How should you calculate power?

---

### Question 8
Explain how the modulo operator (`%`) behaves with negative numerators versus negative denominators.

---

### Question 9
How does C interpret True and False?

---

### Question 10
What is the difference between `=` and `==`? Why is mixing them up considered a dangerous logical error?
