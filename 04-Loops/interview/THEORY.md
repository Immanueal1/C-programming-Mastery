# ❓ Top 10 Technical Interview Theory Questions

> Core technical interview questions covering loop automation, equivalency, `for` header anatomy, `while` vs `do-while`, pre/post increment mechanics, `break`/`continue` rules, and float precision traps.

---

### Question 1
Why do we need loop control instructions in programming? What problem do they solve?

---

### Question 2
Explain the "Loop Equivalency Rule". Are there scenarios where a `for` loop can do something a `while` loop cannot?

---

### Question 3
Break down the anatomy of a `for` loop. What are the three main components inside its signature, and in what exact order do they execute?

---

### Question 4
What is the fundamental operational difference between a `while` loop and a `do-while` loop?

---

### Question 5
When is it mandatory to use a `do-while` loop over a `while` loop? Provide a real-world scenario.

---

### Question 6
What causes an "Infinite Loop"? Give an example of how one can accidentally occur in a `for` loop.

---

### Question 7
Differentiate between Post-Increment (`i++`) and Pre-Increment (`++i`) when used directly inside a loop's condition (e.g., `while(++i < 5)`).

---

### Question 8
Describe the function of the `break` keyword. If placed inside a nested loop, does it stop all loops?

---

### Question 9
How does the `continue` statement alter the flow of a loop? Does it skip the updation phase in a `for` loop?

---

### Question 10
Why is it dangerous to use `==` or `!=` with floating-point iterators in a loop condition?
