# ❓ Top 10 Technical Interview Theory Questions

> Core technical interview questions covering `if-else` vs `switch`, fall-through mechanics, assignment traps, case label types, short-circuit evaluation, and the dangling else problem in C.

---

### Question 1
What is the fundamental difference between an `if-else` ladder and a `switch` statement? When would you prefer one over the other?

---

### Question 2
Explain the "Fall-through" behavior in a `switch` statement. Is it a bug or a feature?

---

### Question 3
Why does the compiler allow `if(x = 5)` instead of throwing an error? What exactly happens in memory?

---

### Question 4
What data types are strictly NOT allowed as cases in a `switch` statement in C?

---

### Question 5
Explain the significance of the "Curly Braces Rule" when dealing with single-line `if` statements.

---

### Question 6
Can a `default` case be placed anywhere in a `switch` statement, or must it be at the bottom? Does it require a `break`?

---

### Question 7
How does the ternary operator (`? :`) differ from a standard `if-else` block? Can it completely replace `if-else` in all scenarios?

---

### Question 8
What is short-circuit evaluation in logical operators (`&&` and `||`), and how does it affect the execution flow?

---

### Question 9
Can you nest a `switch` statement inside an `if` statement, and vice versa?

---

### Question 10
If an `else` is placed after three `if` statements without curly braces, which `if` does the compiler pair the `else` with? (The Dangling Else problem).
